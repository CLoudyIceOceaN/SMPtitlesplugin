⬇️ Download SMPtitlesplugin.jar

version 1.0 · needs SMPmoneyplugin (the installer grabs it for you)

⚡ Fastest way: one command

Terminal → cd into your server folder → paste:

curl -sL https://cloudyiceocean.github.io/SMPtitlesplugin/install.sh | bash

Then restart the server.

For players — /titles

  • The shop shows every title as an enchanted book — hover to preview exactly how it looks and the price
  • Click to buy (with a confirm screen) — it goes on instantly
  • The chest at the bottom = your titles: equip, swap, or remove them any time
  • Titles survive deaths, teleports, and relogs

For the owner — adding titles

The plugin ships with no titles — you add your own in plugins/SMPtitlesplugin/config.yml:

titles:
  king:
    name: "&6&lKING"
    price: 50000
    font: normal
  shadow:
    name: "&8Shadow"
    price: 10000
    font: smallcaps

Fonts: normal, smallcaps (small capital letters — looks the best), fullwidth (wide letters), circled (letters in circles). Save, then type /titles reload in game — no restart. /titles give Steve king gives one for free.
